## Animation

- **Magic Move.** Draw where a diagram starts and where it ends as two pages, then choose Animate ▸ Magic Move to
  Next Page. Shapes with the same anchor or text, or copies made by Duplicate Page, slide, resize, turn and
  recolor into place; whatever is only on one page fades out or in. Scripts can call `dottie.magicMove(page)`.
- **Marching ants.** Animate ▸ March Selected Lines makes the dashes of the selected arrows travel along them,
  for a live stream or the direction of a flow. Scripts can call `dottie.march(id, {speed})`.
- **Springy eases.** Keys can ease with a spring, a bounce or an elastic snap, which overshoot and settle. The
  Drop In transition now bounces to a stop.
- **File ▸ Export Animation** saves the page's animation from the app, which until now needed the command line:
  - a GIF;
  - an animated PNG (like a GIF, in full color);
  - an MP4;
  - a web page with a player;
  - **Lottie**, vector animation for websites and iOS and Android apps. Smooth mode is small and tweened by the
    player. Exact mode keeps every frame as Dottie draws it.

## Editing

- **Back to Select after drawing.** Drawing a shape, arrow, text or note switches back to the Select tool, as in
  Excalidraw, so you can move what you drew straight away. The pencil and eraser stay on. To keep a tool on, turn
  this off in Settings ▸ General ▸ Tools.
- **Clicks are more forgiving.** A click whose finger slides a little no longer nudges the shape or flashes an
  empty marquee. A click that lands just off a shape still selects it. The first click in a window that isn't
  active acts on the canvas, rather than only bringing the window forward.
- Selecting in large drawings is a little quicker.

## Command line, MCP and dottie.js

- `dottie render` writes `.apng`, and `.json` for Lottie, with `--lottie-mode smooth|exact`. `--transparent`
  also applies to both. MCP's `export_animation` takes the same formats.
- dottie.js 0.3 plays marching ants and the new eases. Older players play the new eases as straight moves.
- The [demos](https://phin-tech.github.io/dottie/demos/) now include Magic Move, marching ants and a Lottie
  export played by lottie-web.
