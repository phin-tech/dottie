## Containers and swimlanes

- **Boxes that hold things.** Select some shapes and choose Arrange ▸ Put in Container (⌥⌘K) to put a titled box
  round them: a VPC, a subsystem, a team. It fits round what it holds, moving it moves them, and connectors attach
  to it like any shape. Drop a shape in to add it, drag one out to take it out, and resize the box to keep it at
  that size. Containers nest, and the Layers panel shows what each one holds.
- **Swimlanes.** A container can stack containers inside it as rows or columns, one per lane, each with its title.
- **Mermaid subgraphs** come in as containers, nested as you wrote them, and go back out as subgraphs when you
  export to Mermaid. draw.io swimlanes and containers, and Excalidraw frames, import as containers too.
- For agents: `add_container`, `parent` on `add_shape` and `update_element`, and `clusters` on `build_diagram`, which
  lays out each cluster as one block. Scripts have `dottie.container`, and a `parent` option on shapes, updates and
  `dottie.build`.

## Themes of your own

- **Palettes.** Every theme now has named colors: accent, muted, danger, warning, success and info, lighter on dark
  pages. Click one of the swatches under Stroke or Fill to use a palette color, and it changes when the theme does.
- **Make a theme of the drawing's own** on the Page tab, with its own page color, ink and palette colors, and add
  colors of your own. Changing a palette color recolors everything that uses it.
- For agents: `set_theme`, and every color argument also takes a palette name. Scripts have `dottie.theme()`.

## Arrows go round shapes

- New elbow connectors route round the shapes in their way instead of cutting through them, and keep their plain
  Z or L when nothing is in the way. Turn it off for a connector with Go Round Shapes in the inspector. Existing
  drawings don't change.

## For agents

- `export_png` takes `max_width` for a small preview, `include_image: false` to save the image without sending it
  back, and `elements: "compact"` for a short element list.
- `simulate` has a `{"routes": true}` step: each flow's destination, the connectors it takes, its share and cost,
  and why a flow reaches nothing, so you can check traffic in each state.
- `dottie.connect` and `add_connector` take `stroke`.
- Script type checks no longer report TypeScript 7's strict-mode noise in plain JavaScript. They group problems by
  kind and show mistakes in dottie calls first.
