## Symbols

- **Symbols, like Flash's movie clips.** Select some shapes and choose Arrange ▸ Convert to Symbol (F8). They become
  one drawing, defined once with its own animation, that you can place again and again. Every copy plays that
  animation on its own clock: over and over, once, or standing still, and from any point in it, so a row of status
  lights can blink out of step. Copies move, resize, turn, fade and take keyframes like any shape.
- **Edit Symbol.** Double-click a copy (or Arrange ▸ Edit Symbol) to change the symbol itself, with its own
  timeline; every copy follows. A bar over the canvas leads back to the page.
- **Break Apart** (⌘B) turns a copy back into plain shapes.
- **Buttons.** Tick Button in a symbol's inspector section and Dottie gives it up, over and down scenes; Edit
  Symbol to draw how it looks at each, and add a hit scene to set where it takes clicks. Its copies show up in
  every export, and on the web change under the pointer and while pressed. Give a copy a click like any shape.
- **Play when clicked.** A plain symbol's copy set to play "When clicked" rests at its start and plays through
  when clicked in `<dottie-player>` (the web page export, drawn ahead of time, shows it at rest).
- Copying a copy to another page or drawing brings its symbol along, and so does Magic Move. A symbol added to a
  library plays wherever it is placed.
- Every export draws symbols: PNG, SVG, PDF, GIF, APNG, MP4, Lottie and the web page.
- **Files with symbols need this version.** Dottie 0.5 and earlier can't open them, and dottie.js 0.5 draws each
  copy as a labelled placeholder.

## Clicks on the web

- **When Clicked on the Web**, a new inspector section, makes a shape a button in File ▸ Export Animation's web
  page and in `<dottie-player>`. A click plays a scene on to the next one, or opens a link in a new tab. A diagram
  can then wait for the reader to pick which request to follow, as in the new
  [demo](https://phin-tech.github.io/dottie/demos/).
- Give the shape a hover color too. It shows under the pointer and when the shape is reached with Tab; Enter or
  Space clicks it. With reduced motion, a click jumps straight to where its scene stops.
- Only `http`, `https` and `mailto` links are followed. Nothing in a drawing ever runs as code, so a drawing from
  anyone is safe to play on your site.
- In `<dottie-player>`, a shape linked to a shape on another page shows that page when clicked.

## Command line, MCP and dottie.js

- MCP: `make_symbol` (with `button: true`, the page's up, over, down and hit scenes move into it) and `place_symbol`; `update_element` takes `click_scene`, `click_url`, `hover_stroke`,
  `hover_fill` and `hover_opacity`, and `playback` and `offset` for a symbol's copy.
- Scripts: `dottie.symbol` (with `{button: true}`), `dottie.place`, `dottie.symbols` and `dottie.breakApart`; `dottie.update` takes `click`,
  `hover`, `playback` and `offset`.
- dottie.js 0.6 plays symbols and clicks. `<dottie-player>` fires a cancelable `action` event before each click and
  has `activate(elementId)`.
- The web page export is the same file every time for the same drawing.
