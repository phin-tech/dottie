# Dottie

**Diagrams that look hand-drawn, on your Mac, and your AI assistant can draw them for you.**

Sketch a flowchart, a system diagram or a whiteboard idea in seconds. Switch to a clean, precise look when it
needs to go in a document. Or just ask Claude to draw it, and watch the shapes appear. Then make it move.

That animation is the Dottie drawing itself, played in your browser by
[dottie.js](https://github.com/phin-tech/dottie/tree/main/web): press play, drag the slider, or step with the
arrow keys.

## What it does

- **Draw it yourself** with shapes, connectors that stay attached, sticky notes in Markdown, pictures, pages,
  links, layers and themes. Sketchy or clean.
- **Ask your assistant to draw it.** Dottie works with Claude and other assistants that support MCP: describe a
  diagram and watch it appear in the window, one undoable change at a time.
- **Make it move.** Build a diagram up shape by shape, send requests along its arrows, pan and zoom a camera
  around it, and step through it scene by scene. Export a GIF, an MP4, or a web page like the ones here.
- **Bring diagrams in and send them out:** Mermaid, Excalidraw, draw.io and SVG in; PNG, SVG, PDF, Mermaid
  and HTML out.

The [README](https://github.com/phin-tech/dottie#readme) has the details, from installing to keyboard
shortcuts.
